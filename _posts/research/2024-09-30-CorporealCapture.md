title: Corporeal Capture - The Rhetoric of Boundaries in Procedurality
topic: Object Oriented Programming, Game Studies, Software Studies
description: This paper studies how object-oriented paradigm, prevalent in game engineering, can embed ideological assumptions in building a game, where certain representation of world is easier than others. It studies Crusader Kings III and Civilization VI to talk about how ablist and nationalistic representations are downstream of its object-oriented representational scheme.
